Why I Keep Writing

Writing has always been a contradiction for me. A space of silence that somehow speaks louder than my voice ever could.

As a child, I avoided language. I hid from it. But over time, writing became the only place where my thoughts felt allowed to exist fully.

I continue to write not because I always want to—but because not writing feels like ignoring a part of myself that refuses to be quiet.
