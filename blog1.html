I've been asked what I want to explore with this blog. Being that I had never thought of writing one until I enrolled in this class, I honestly have no idea. What I do know is something has been on my mind lately. I recently applied to graduate school at Emerson University, and ever since I first made that decision to pursue a masters degree, the prospect of teaching at the college level has been encroaching on my mind, swallowing whole whatever alternative paths I was previously considering, and I don't mind it.


Writing changed my life, and I don't say that lightly---in fact---I'll say it again. Writing changed my life. If I hadn't become a writer, I don't what where, or what, or who I'd be. Likely a far sadder individual with a similarly sad, empty looking bookshelf.


When I think back to who I was when I first started writing, versus who I am now, that change alone is enough fuel to drive to me teach. To be someone who gets the opportunity to bring the sublime craft of writing into a persons life, to revitalize and nourish their worlds with literature, help them hone and explore their craft---I can't think of a better way to spend my life.


So what do I want to explore with this blog? The act of writing, immortalizing our thoughts on the page, the effect teachers have on our lives, the effect students have on their teacher's lives, how reading and reading intently nourishes our minds and souls---these are all things I want to explore, unweave, and rebuild again in this space---alongside many other things I likely haven't thought of yet.


But for now, this is just a place for contemplation for you and I. Thanks for taking some time to think with me. I hope you'll return to think some more.


Until next time,

Adam Campney
